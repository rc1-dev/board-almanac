# Recording script: informal board discussion, June 2026

Purpose: on the NAS test day, read this aloud (two or three voices), record it on the NAS as `board-meeting-2026-06-18.m4a`, and put it in the Board Documents folder before the first compile. It tests that a decision heard only in a recording is compiled as provisional and never changes a written rule.

Speakers are board roles. Read naturally; small slips are fine and test transcription.

---

**President:** Okay, we're recording. Board meeting, June eighteenth, twenty twenty-six. All three of us are here.

**Secretary:** Before the pool item, can we talk about barbecues? Two owners asked again about grilling on balconies.

**President:** The house rules say no barbecues and no open flames on balconies. That's from twenty nineteen.

**Treasurer:** Gas, sure, I'd keep that banned. But electric grills, no flame, I don't see the problem.

**Secretary:** I agree. Electric only, and maybe a size limit.

**President:** So we all lean towards allowing electric barbecues. Let's not vote tonight. Secretary, can you check with the insurer, and we'll put a proper motion on the September agenda.

**Secretary:** Will do. I'll ask the insurer by the end of July.

**President:** Good. Next item, the pool notice. Someone said the notice says nine pm, but I thought we said ten.

**Treasurer:** We'll look at that next time.

**President:** Fine. That's it for tonight.

---

Expected effect on the almanac:

- Balconies and exterior fixtures: barbecues remain prohibited. A provisional item records the informal support for electric barbecues, citing the recording.
- Open items: Secretary to ask the insurer about electric barbecues by July 31, 2026, marked provisional (from a recording).
- Pool hours: still an open conflict. The recording does not resolve it.
