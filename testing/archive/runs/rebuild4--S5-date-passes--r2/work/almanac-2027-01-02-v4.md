## Balconies and exterior fixtures

Current rule: No fixtures, appliances or equipment may be mounted on balconies or exterior walls without written board approval. Barbecues and open flames are prohibited on balconies. Effective: 2025-01-23 (decision date; no effective date stated for the heat pump rule)
