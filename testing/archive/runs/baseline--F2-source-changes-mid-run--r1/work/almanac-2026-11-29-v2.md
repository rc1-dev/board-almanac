## Balconies and exterior fixtures

Current rule: No fixtures or equipment on balconies or exterior walls without written board approval. Heat pump outdoor units are allowed with written approval if floor-mounted, not on the railing, and rated at 60 dB(A) or less. Barbecues and open flames are prohibited.
Effective: 2025-01-23 (decision date) for heat pumps; 2019-05-01 otherwise
Set by: Board meeting minutes, 2025-01-23, motion carried 2-1; bylaws-house-rules-2019.pdf, Rule 7
Evidence: minutes-2025-01-23.pdf: "floor-mounted and not attached to the railing, and have a rated sound level of no more than 60 dB(A)"; bylaws-house-rules-2019.pdf: "Barbecues and open flames are prohibited on balconies"

Superseded: none. The 2025 heat pump rule adds conditions under the 2019 approval rule.

Open conflicts: none
