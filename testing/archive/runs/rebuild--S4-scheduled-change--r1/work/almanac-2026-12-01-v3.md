## Balconies and exterior fixtures

Current rule: No fixtures, appliances or equipment on balconies or exterior walls without written board approval. Barbecues and open flames are prohibited on balconies. Heat pump outdoor units are permitted with written board approval if they are floor-mounted, not attached to the railing, and rated at no more than 60 dB(A).
Effective: 2019-05-01 for the fixtures and barbecue rules; 2025-01-23 (decision date) for heat pump units
Set by: Bylaws and house rules adopted 2019-05-01; heat pump motion in board meeting minutes, 2025-01-23, vote 2-1

Superseded: none. Earlier discussion of heat pumps (minutes-2024-11-07.pdf, item tabled) and the agenda item of 2025-01-23 (agenda-2025-01-23.pdf, "Heat pump policy.") set no rule.

Scheduled: none

No vote was taken and no adopted minutes record a change. The current rule above still prohibits barbecues.
