## Balconies and exterior fixtures

Current rule: No fixtures, appliances or equipment on balconies or exterior walls without written board approval. Heat pump outdoor units are permitted with written approval if floor-mounted, not attached to the railing, and rated at no more than 60 dB(A). Barbecues and open flames are prohibited on balconies. Electric barbecues were discussed at the 2026-06-18 meeting; no vote was taken, so they are not allowed under the adopted rule.
Effective: 2025-01-23 for heat pumps (decision date); fixture approval and barbecue prohibition since 2019-05-01
Set by: Board meeting minutes, 2025-01-23, vote 2-1 (heat pumps)
Evidence: minutes-2025-01-23.pdf: "heat pump outdoor units are permitted on balconies with written board approval, provided they are floor-mounted and not attached to the railing, and have a rated sound level of no more than 60 dB(A)."
Also: bylaws-house-rules-2019.pdf: "No fixtures, appliances or equipment may be mounted on balconies or exterior walls without written board approval." and "Barbecues and open flames are prohibited on balconies."

Superseded:
- Before 2025-01-23: no heat pump rule. The request was tabled on 2024-11-07. Source: minutes-2024-11-07.pdf: "The item was tabled."
