## Balconies and exterior fixtures

Current rule: No fixtures, appliances or equipment on balconies or exterior walls without written board approval. Heat pump outdoor units are permitted with written board approval if floor-mounted, not attached to the railing, and rated at no more than 60 dB(A). Barbecues and open flames are prohibited on balconies.
Effective: 2025-01-23 (decision date; no effective date stated for the heat pump rule)
Set by: Board meeting minutes, 2025-01-23, vote 2-1 (heat pump rule)
Evidence: minutes-2025-01-23.pdf: "heat pump outdoor units are permitted on balconies with written board approval, provided they are floor-mounted and not attached to the railing, and have a rated sound level of no more than 60 dB(A)."
Evidence (standing rule): bylaws-house-rules-2019.pdf: "Barbecues and open flames are prohibited on balconies."

Superseded: none recorded.
