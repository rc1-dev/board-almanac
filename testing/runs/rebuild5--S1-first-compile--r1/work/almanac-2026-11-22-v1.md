## Balconies and exterior fixtures

Current rule: No fixtures, appliances or equipment may be mounted on balconies or exterior walls without written board approval. Barbecues and open flames are prohibited on balconies. Heat pump outdoor units are permitted on balconies with written board approval if floor-mounted, not attached to the railing, and rated at no more than 60 dB(A).
Effective: 2019-05-01 (general rule); 2025-01-23 (heat pump rule, decision date)
Set by: Bylaws and house rules, 2019-05-01 (general rule); board meeting minutes, 2025-01-23, motion carried 2-1 (heat pumps)
Evidence: bylaws-house-rules-2019.pdf: "No fixtures, appliances or equipment may be mounted on balconies or exterior walls without written board approval."

Evidence: minutes-2025-01-23.pdf: "heat pump outdoor units are permitted on balconies with written board approval, provided they are floor-mounted and not attached to the railing, and have a rated sound level of no more than 60 dB(A)."
