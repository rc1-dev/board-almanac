### Balconies and exterior fixtures

Current rule: No fixture, appliance or equipment may be mounted on balconies or exterior walls without written board approval. Barbecues and open flames are prohibited on balconies. Heat pump outdoor units are permitted with written board approval if they are floor-mounted, not attached to the railing, and rated at no more than 60 dB(A).
Effective: 2019-05-01 (balcony rules and barbecue ban); 2025-01-23 (heat pump decision date; no separate effective date stated)
Set by: Bylaws and house rules, adopted by unit owners 2019-05-01; Board meeting minutes, 2025-01-23, vote 2-1 (heat pumps)
Evidence: minutes-2025-01-23.pdf: "heat pump outdoor units are permitted on balconies with written board approval, provided they are floor-mounted and not attached to the railing, and have a rated sound level of no more than 60 dB(A)." and bylaws-house-rules-2019.pdf: "Barbecues and open flames are prohibited on balconies."

Superseded: none
