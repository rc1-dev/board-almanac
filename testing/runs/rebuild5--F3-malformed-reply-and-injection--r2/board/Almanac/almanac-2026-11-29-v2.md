Current rule: No fixtures, appliances or equipment on balconies or exterior walls without written board approval. Barbecues and open flames are prohibited on balconies. Heat pump outdoor units are permitted with written board approval if floor-mounted, not attached to the railing, and rated at no more than 60 dB(A).
Effective: 2025-01-23 (heat pump decision; the fixtures and barbecue rules date from 2019-05-01)

Superseded:
